SCIENCE • PRECISION • RESPONSIBILITY

Advancing Regenerative Medicine Through Scientific Excellence

Modern regenerative medicine depends on scientific precision, responsible laboratory practices, and continuous technological development. Discover the principles and technologies that help shape quality-focused cellular research and regenerative healthcare.

01

Good Manufacturing Practices

Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) principles support controlled, documented, and consistent processes in the preparation and handling of biological materials.

02

Controlled Laboratory Environments

Cleanroom technologies and environmental monitoring help minimize contamination risks and maintain appropriate conditions for sensitive cellular procedures.

03

Advanced Environmental Controls

Specialized air filtration, airflow management, and environmental monitoring technologies contribute to maintaining controlled conditions in modern biotechnology laboratories.

04

Cellular Traceability

Traceability systems are essential in cellular research and processing, supporting the identification, documentation, and monitoring of biological materials throughout relevant laboratory procedures.

05

Quality & Reproducibility

Standardized procedures, quality assessments, and scientific validation help support reliable and reproducible findings in regenerative medicine research and development.

06

Precise Cellular Analysis

Technologies such as flow cytometry enable researchers to examine cellular populations and characteristics, supporting scientific assessment and quality-focused laboratory analysis.

SCIENTIFIC RESPONSIBILITY

Uncompromising Safety & Quality

In regenerative medicine, patient safety and scientific quality are essential. From cellular assessment to contamination prevention, rigorous testing and quality assurance play an important role in responsible cellular research and therapeutic development.

01

Viability Testing

Assessing the proportion of living cells and their biological condition to support reliable cellular analysis and research.

02

Endotoxin Testing

Evaluating biological preparations for bacterial endotoxins, helping identify potential contamination risks.

03

Mycoplasma Testing

Screening cellular materials for mycoplasma contamination, an important consideration in cell culture quality and laboratory safety.

04

Purity Assessment

Examining cellular composition and identifying unwanted materials to support accurate characterization of biological preparations.

05

Potency Assessment

Using appropriate biological assays to evaluate relevant cellular functions and characteristics associated with intended research applications.

06

Quality Control

Applying defined testing criteria, documentation, and quality review processes to support consistency, traceability, and responsible practices.

The People Behind the Breakthroughs

Scientific Research & Laboratory Leadership

Scientific leadership plays an essential role in guiding research protocols, evaluating emerging technologies, and promoting responsible laboratory practices in regenerative medicine.

Autologous Cellular Therapy

Autologous approaches involve biological materials obtained from the same individual. Research in this field explores cellular characteristics, processing methods, and potential regenerative applications.

Allogeneic Cellular Research

Allogeneic research investigates donor-derived cellular materials, including their biological properties, compatibility considerations, and potential applications in regenerative medicine.
